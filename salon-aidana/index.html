Version françaiseHow it works
  • In the French version, your house starts empty. Only you, Léo and the pets are here.
  • To bring an object back, type its name in French in the box under the room, for example le canapé, then press Enter.
  • It has to be an object you already have in the English version.
  • Accents and articles are optional, but you'll be shown the correct spelling (le, la, les…).
  • Don't know a word? Open your 📖 dictionary: every object of your house is there, in English and in French.
  • Here you can't move or store objects, only find them again. Your English house doesn't change.
  • Switch back to English anytime with the button at the top.
